About BIOROOT

BIOROOT is a charity awareness platform dedicated to promoting charities, NGOs and non-profit organisations throughout Africa. Our goal is to increase awareness, encourage community involvement and make it easier for people to discover organisations that are creating meaningful change.

Our Mission

BIOROOT exists to bridge the gap between communities and charitable organisations. We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to contribute towards solving important issues such as poverty, environmental conservation, education, healthcare and humanitarian relief.

Our Vision

We envision an Africa where every charity has access to the visibility and support needed to positively impact lives. BIOROOT aims to become the leading platform connecting volunteers, donors, governments and organisations working together for sustainable development.

Challenges We Help Address

🌍 Global Warming

Climate change continues to affect communities, wildlife and ecosystems. BIOROOT promotes organisations working towards environmental conservation and sustainability.

♻ Pollution

Pollution threatens human health and biodiversity. We support charities focused on recycling, waste reduction and environmental clean-up initiatives.

📚 Education

Access to quality education remains a challenge in many communities. BIOROOT raises awareness about organisations improving educational opportunities across Africa.
